## Page-by-page findings

### Page 45 – Inventory quantity control and start of stocktaking
**PARTIAL – explicit problem categories missing.**
Present:
- Inventory means cargo held in the warehouse and quantities should be checked regularly.
- Stock tags / cargo tags (さし札・荷札), receiving number and cargo information.
- Update tags when goods enter/leave; count groups separately by receiving or lot number.
- Keep tags visible and storage locations organised.
- A general instruction to report problems immediately to the work-management responsible person.
Missing from the page 45 DOCX text:
- The source's three specific reportable problem categories and explanations:
  1. Quantity mismatch (数が合わない): number on the stock tag differs from actual quantity.
  2. Damaged cargo (貨物が傷んでいる): cargo is broken or dirty/contaminated.
  3. Something unusual (何かおかしい): an abnormal event or condition has occurred.
The DOCX contains only a generic “report any of these problems” sentence, without the actual list. Add the three-row bilingual table on page 45.

### Page 46 – Stocktaking and sample-release start

### Page 49 – Fire prevention and flood/water-damage preparation
**MAJOR GAP – source flood-preparedness section missing.**

Missing:

- Keep sandbags (土のう) ready in warehouses at risk of water ingress.
- Know the emergency response procedures for flooding.
- Use a hazard map (ハザードマップ) to identify dangerous areas and assess nearby river risk.
Add the full bilingual flood-preparedness and hazard-map section at the page 49 position. Do not count the generic water-leak inspection on page 48 as coverage of flood preparedness.

### Page 50 – Emergency response
**SUBSTANTIALLY COVERED; verify complete English/Sinhala pairing.**
Present:
- Know emergency exits and evacuation routes; never block them with cargo/pallets; do not use elevators in emergencies; follow green exit-sign arrows.
- Earthquake response: move to safe place due to falling cargo, protect oneself, evacuate after shaking stops, report safe status.
- Elevator earthquake procedure and emergency buzzer/intercom if trapped.
- Fire response: raise alarm, use extinguisher only if fire is small, stop work and evacuate when alarm sounds.
- Extinguisher operation: pull pin, aim nozzle, squeeze lever.
- Cold-store power failure: use portable/forklift light, move to exit and manually open door.
The corresponding DOCX has bilingual step paragraphs and a complete English/Sinhala extinguisher sequence. Check final formatting to ensure each step is displayed as a matched pair.
