### Page 115 – Basic forklift rules and driving safety
**SUBSTANTIALLY COVERED at text level.**

### Page 116 – Warehouse driving and loading/unloading procedure
**SUBSTANTIALLY COVERED; verify final sequence layout against source.**
Present:

- Eight-step loading/unloading sequence with fork alignment, 5–10 cm initial lift, 10–20 cm draw-back, safe travel height, placement and high-level pallet retrieval precautions.
The bilingual text is present. Final proof should confirm all eight steps appear in source order and each English step has its Sinhala counterpart.
