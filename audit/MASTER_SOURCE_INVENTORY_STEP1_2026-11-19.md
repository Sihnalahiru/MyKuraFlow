## 2. Master syllabus structure from the source TOC

### Part 1 — 物流倉庫の基本 (Warehouse fundamentals), printed pages 9–53
- I. 倉庫の種類 (warehouse types), p.9:
  - 普通倉庫; 冷蔵倉庫; 水面倉庫; トランクルーム; 特別の倉庫; temperature-based warehouse classification.
- II. 倉庫に関係する設備・用語 (warehouse facilities and terminology), p.13:
  - Building facilities: floor, walls, ceiling, fire-fighting equipment, storage equipment, other facilities.
  - Cargo-moving equipment: cargo elevator, vertical automatic conveyor, forklift, picking forklift, hand lift, carts, electric/automatic transport equipment, conveyors, sorters, cranes/mobile cranes (p.19).
  - Warehouse equipment: pallets; palletizer/de-palletizer; vanning/devanning systems; packing/banding/stretch machines; slinging tools; handy terminals; needle detectors; vacuum cleaners; platform scales; dimension/weight measuring devices (p.26).
  - Vehicles/machinery: maritime containers, railway containers, trucks, power gates/tailgate lifters (p.30).
- III. 倉庫業務の基本 (warehouse operations fundamentals), p.35:
  - Warehouse/cold-storage deposit terms; storage charges and handling charges; worker responsibilities including duty of care and confidentiality.
- IV. 保管の品質管理 (storage quality control), p.41:
  - Cargo-appropriate storage; co-storage precautions for odorous cargo; effective space use; warehouse housekeeping; other controls.
- V. 在庫管理 (inventory control), p.45:
  - Inventory quantity management; stocktaking; sample dispatch; disposal/destruction.
- VI. 防犯・災害対策 (security and disaster prevention), p.48:
  - Roof leaks; rodent damage; door opening/closing; fire prevention; flood damage; emergency response for earthquake, fire and power failure (through p.50).
- Appendix: pallet types, p.51–53.

### Part 2 — 作業マニュアル (Work manual), printed pages 54–89
- I. 作業に必要な知識 (knowledge required for work), p.54:
  - PPE/workwear: work clothes, safety vest, helmet, safety shoes, gloves.
  - Documents and labels: inbound/outbound documents, labels, care marks and GHS labels.
  - Units: length, area, volume/capacity, weight, temperature/humidity, time/calendar, illuminance.
  - Japanese counters/classifiers (助数詞), p.61.
- II. 作業の基本 (basic operations), p.64:
  - Inbound work: preparation, vehicle guidance, maritime-container devanning, unloading, inbound inspection, put-away, location/identification tags.
  - Outbound work, p.72: preparation, picking, packing, sorting/inspection, loading, vehicle guidance, handover.
- III. 流通加工 (distribution processing), p.78:
  - Labeling, tagging, insertion, cutting, repacking, kitting, package wrapping, outer packing, unpacking, band cutting, PP strapping, stretch wrapping, wooden cases/crates, returns refurbishment, needle inspection, sewing, cleaning.
  - Work planning, preparation, execution, progress management and completion (pp.84–86).
- Appendix: care marks, pp.87–89.

### Part 3 — 物流倉庫の安全衛生 (Warehouse safety and health), printed pages 90–113
- I. 労働時間と健康管理 (working hours and health management), p.90:
  - Working hours; occupational accidents (p.91); daily health management, injury/illness prevention, heatstroke (p.92–94).
  - Safe-work basics and PPE (p.95); manual handling, lifting posture and back-injury prevention (p.96); warehouse work hazards and safe practices (p.97–98).
  - On-site safety: signs/notices, aisles and work floors (p.99–101).
  - KYT hazard prediction training (p.102–103).
  - 5S: seiri, seiton, seisou, seiketsu, shitsuke (p.104–105).
  - Abnormal weather and special work; hazardous/harmful substances (p.106–108).
- Appendix: GHS labels/pictograms, pp.109–113.

### Part 4 — フォークリフト作業基準 (Forklift work standards), printed pages 114–122
- Pre-start inspection and operating checks; head guard (p.114–115).
- Basic precautions: identification, routes/restricted areas, prohibited driving behaviours (p.115).
- Driving precautions: sudden controls, turning, carrying pallets, pre-drive checks, warehouse travel, floor joints, obstructed forward view and reversing (p.115–116).
- Basic loading/unloading sequence (p.117).
- Loading/unloading precautions and driver communication; suitable equipment; load stability and long cargo (p.118).
- Stacking: cargo shape/strength, cartons, floor load limits, fork insertion, final stability check, empty pallet stacking limit (p.118–119).
- Parking: designated parking, five fork-stop principles, leaving the operator seat (p.119).
- Cargo-specific procedures: pallet cargo, flexible container bags, roll cargo using clamp lift (p.120–121).
- Container operations: vanning/devanning, load-collapse risk, moving/connecting belt conveyors, emergency stop (p.122).
